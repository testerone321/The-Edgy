# Survival Mode

## ⚡ How It Works

Survival mode never stops. There are no phases, no end goal — just you against the device.

Stroking runs **continuously**. Speed is determined by the **Tension slider** you control in real time.

- Slide **left** → faster
- Slide **right** → slower

When you can't take it anymore, press **EDGE** to trigger a cooldown pause.

## ⏸️ Cooldown Pauses

- **First edge:** 10 seconds of rest.
- **Each subsequent edge:** 2 seconds shorter (minimum: 5 seconds).
- The more you edge, the less relief you get.

## 📈 Pressure Spike

Every 60 seconds without pressing EDGE, your speed envelope shifts up by **+5%** — and it stacks.

Pressing EDGE resets the spike back to zero.

## ⚙️ Configuration

Use the **Configure Settings** button to set the stroke range (min/max position). Speed is always controlled live via the Tension slider.
